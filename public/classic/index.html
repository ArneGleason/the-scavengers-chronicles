The Scavenger's Chronicles

The Scavenger lives in a house where the 1950s interior never blinked, the basement never surrendered, and every newspaper stack is apparently part of a filing system.

He owns synthesizers, mixers, rack gear, mystery power supplies, and enough cables to restrain a small elevator. None of this has produced music, but he remains confident the masterpiece is one adapter away.

The first objective is simple: find the obscure DIN sync cable in the basement hoard and bring it to the synth altar. After that, surely, forty years of composition will begin immediately.

The best thing about this game is that it is very short, which is also what people say about his completed songs, if any are ever found.

Controls: move with WASD or arrows. Press E, Space, or Enter to inspect, pick up, and deliver. M opens missions. C opens the optional checklist. R drops cargo. F uses it. Every minute, soup becomes more important than your plans. The hairspray can is not a mechanic. It is a cry for help in aerosol form.

Good luck. Try not to trip over a cable he calls "load-bearing."
