# Art Bible

v0, 28 September 2026. It covers the chosen direction (ligne claire comic in 3D, fixed isometric camera) and the rules every asset, shader and screen should follow. Character-specific detail lives in `characters/`.

## North star

**Any random frame should read as a ligne claire comic panel.** That means clean ink lines of even weight, flat colour, two-tone shading with tinted shadows, and print texture only where it earns its place.

Touchstones: Hergé's Tintin, Untitled Goose Game, Richard Scarry and Chris Ware cutaways, MAD Magazine background gags, and the Mega Vegas Elvis comics for the fantasy register.

## One look, in-engine

Decided 28 September 2026: **every cutscene and comic panel is rendered in-engine**, from the same 3D scene with the same toon shading and ink. That covers inspect close-ups, barks that freeze into panels, the Big Wanda reveal and game-over, the soup photo, the Research Log and the Vegas finale. There are no separately drawn illustrations, so the game has one consistent look, and every panel can animate.

What makes an in-engine shot read as a comic panel rather than a game camera:

- **Framing.** Panels are cut into the screen with cream gutters and ink borders, one to three per page. A shot can break the border for emphasis (a hand, a flying cable, Big Wanda).
- **Lenses.** Play is orthographic. Cutscenes may switch to a narrow-lens perspective camera (10–20°) for depth, and to strong close-ups the gameplay camera never uses.
- **Timing.** Poses hold "on twos" (12 fps) and panels freeze on the key pose. Motion lines and a sound-effect lettering pop sell the moment between panels.
- **Lettering.** Balloons and caption boxes as in the lettering section, placed per panel.
- **Print finish.** Line boil on, a little more halftone, and misregistration on the big beats.
- **Staging.** Characters face the camera more than real people would, the way comic actors do. Props are placed for the shot, not for the level.

Mega Vegas Elvis stays inside the world: comic covers on the stacks, the fridge photo, the Vegas marquee in the finale. The covers are small textures in the ligne claire style, not a second art style.

## Line

- **Ink colour:** warm black `#1e1a18`, never pure black.
- **Weights at 1080p:**

  | Line | Weight |
  |---|---|
  | Silhouettes | 2.5 px |
  | Object boundaries | 1.75 px |
  | Creases | 1 px |

- **Characters and interactables** get silhouette weight all the way round.
- **Background clutter** gets "coloured ink" (a darkened version of the object's own colour) at 1 to 1.5 px, so the junk recedes.
- **Interior detail is painted into textures**, not generated by the shader: wood grain, knit ribbing, synth panel seams, newspaper columns.
- **Line boil** (a slight 8 Hz wobble) is off during play and on only during panel freezes and cutscenes. Constant boil is tiring.

## Colour and light

- **Flat albedo.** No painted lighting in the textures except ambient occlusion and grime.
- **Light bands:** two, plus an optional third for ground contact:

  | Band | Use |
  |---|---|
  | Lit | Everywhere the key light reaches |
  | Shade | Everywhere it doesn't |
  | Core shadow (optional) | Ground contact only |

- **Key light:** one fixed direction per zone, from the top left of the screen, so shadows fall down and to the right as they do in most comics.
- **Shadows are tinted, never grey:**

  | Zone | Shadow tint |
  |---|---|
  | Estate | Mint-teal |
  | Basement | Deep blue-violet, with orange bounce from the synth LEDs |
  | The Route | Cool slate |
  | Landfill | Olive |
  | Factory | Umber |
  | Airfield | Violet dusk |

- **Rim light** on characters and interactables only.
- **Shine shapes** (flat white specular) only on glass, chrome, vinyl, synth panels and Bill's sunglasses.
- **Value grouping:**
  - Background dressing stays within roughly 40 to 75% value.
  - Characters and interactables use the full range, from ink black to paper white.
  - Every zone must pass a greyscale check.
- **Reserved colour.** Interactables get a "salvage gold" (`#f2b632`) outline glow and idle bounce. Nothing else in the world uses that hue.

## Print texture

| Texture | Where | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Halftone dots | Mid-tones on large surfaces (walls, floors, skies); also stubble, rust and grime patches, Vegas skies | Locked to the world, with the dot size constant on screen |
| Hatching | Core shadows under furniture, crevices, the landfill and factory | This is how "grime" reads without noise |
| Paper grain | Full screen | Barely visible |
| Misregistration (colour offset) | Impacts, fantasy moments, the finale | Never during ordinary play |

## Camera and composition

- **Orthographic, fixed yaw at 45°, pitch 30–35°.** Choose the pitch from how the estate rooms compose.
- **Compose each room like a panel.** Give it one focal prop, a clear walking lane, and junk grouped into masses rather than scattered.
- **Dollhouse cutaways.** Roofs lift and camera-facing walls drop to capped stubs when Bill enters. The basement is revealed by slicing the ground floor away.
- **Props near Bill** fade through a Ben-Day dither. Behind anything else, his inked silhouette shows through.
- **Zoom levels:**

  | Zoom | Bill's height (1080p) | Used for |
  |---|---|---|
  | Game | ~110 px | Ordinary play |
  | Close | ~220 px | Interiors, dialogue |
  | Panel | Framed | Inspect moments and cutscenes |

## Lettering

All faces are on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, so they can ship with the game.

| Use | Face | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Speech balloons | **Shantell Sans**, weight 600–700, with a little of its "bounce" axis for hand-lettered irregularity | White oval, 2.5 px ink stroke, tapered tail to the speaker's mouth, mixed case |
| Narrator captions | **Courier Prime** | `caption` yellow box with a 2 px ink border, sentence case. The typewriter face nods to Bill's typewriter and matches the MVE caption on "Bill unleashes his legal department." |
| Sound effects | **Bangers** as a base; signature effects (CLANK!, SLURP, BONK) hand-drawn as SVG | Outlined, coloured, rotated ±10°, popping in with overshoot |
| Menus and settings | **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** | Plain and readable; kept out of the comic layer |

Blambot fonts are out: their free licence excludes games.

## Set dressing and signs

- **Bill labels things.** Masking tape and marker everywhere in his spaces: BILL ONLY, DO NOT, EXHIBIT A, PROJECT (1987).
- **A running sign format** across every zone: "X KEEPS ME SANE (BARELY)" and "SAME X DIFFERENT Y" on mugs, napkin holders, fridge notes and shop signs.
- **Signs can be saltier than dialogue.** The reference has SAME SHIT DIFFERENT DAY on a napkin holder.
- **Parody brands only.** No real logos or trade dress on synths, appliances, newspapers or the jet.

## Readability checklist

Every zone and asset must pass these before it's called done.

1. Bill reads at 110 px tall: hair curtains, glasses and stoop.
2. Every interactable is distinct from the dressing at a glance.
3. No background prop out-contrasts an interactable.
4. The zone passes a greyscale check.
5. No text is baked into 3D except signs and labels.
6. It still reads at the low quality tier (75% render scale, no normal-based lines).
