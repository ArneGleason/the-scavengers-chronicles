# Story

Story plan, v0, 30 September 2026. It covers the plot from the neighbourhood errands to Vegas, the memory ledger behind it, and the backlog of errands not yet built.

**Source:** the round-2 outside brainstorm (ChatGPT, fed [briefs/story-brainstorm.md](briefs/story-brainstorm.md)), sorted and trimmed. Ledger rows are premises from earlier conversations with the friends' circle. Everything marked **[new]** was invented in the brainstorm. Numbers like **L6** refer to ledger rows.

**Rules:**
- Only fictional stand-ins for real people outside the circle of friends. Kevin, the students, the joggers and the Lug Nutz are all invented.
- No real addresses, street names or businesses.
- The tone rules in [characters/bill.md](characters/bill.md) apply: the joke is on his logic and obsessions, never on his health, money, age, loneliness or looks.

## Corrections that stand

- The house's age comes from never replacing anything. It should feel congested, improvised and still growing.
- Bill's teaching subject, school and years are unknown. The vinegar lessons are established comic material; everything else about his job stays open.
- No prog album list was recovered. The two titles in LIFE LESSONS! are placeholders.
- *Captain Caffeine*'s first page stays excellent. The superhero lore supplies imagery and props, not the page's contents.
- Both grievances coexist: Bill objects when he can't find himself in Rob's comic, and objects when Rob uses him.
- The photo replies don't have to admire him. **One useful reply among the complaints** is enough to move the story.

## The road to the finale

The story turns when **one genuinely useful reply** arrives among the photo-card complaints. Kevin's last favour then sends Bill to the airfield. Kevin thinks it's a clever way to get a quiet afternoon; Bill thinks it's his media career starting.

**His small victories are real:** he writes eight good bars, his first page finds a reader, and he really was in Rob's comic. He doesn't have to admit his methods were excessive.

The Vegas reveal is about **credit and permission**, never his appearance.

This supersedes the draft beats in `GAME_BIBLE.md` where they differ: the third note as the hook, "the bridge remains in committee", page ten and Mina's text are new.

## Questions for the user

These came back from the brainstorm. They're facts only the user can supply, and each one would make the material more personal.

1. **What did Bill actually teach, and roughly when?** Is there a classroom routine or phrase you remember?
2. **Which prog bands and albums does he insist people know?** Three to five titles would make LIFE LESSONS! and the Album Index his own. The placeholders now are *Close to the Edge* and *Tarkus*.
3. **What is actually on *Captain Caffeine*'s excellent first page?** Do Pedro or any coffee gadgets appear, or are they from separate development?
4. **What was the Vinegar Wolf story?** Only the name and a shadow-puppet connection were recovered.
5. **Which Cheesecloth Period details are canon?** The phrase is certain; the school bottling and "Whipple's Character Vinegar" began as brainstorm elaborations.
6. **What is the *Whiskey Runner Rob* premise?** Rob's motorcycle-and-trailer entrance is a guess until then. The character bible's hook 4 (a cruiser with a parrot dome) may already answer it.
7. **Is Kevin wholly fictional?** The game treats him as invented.
8. **Is there a favourite real "tiny achievement treated as historic"?** It could become the best errand and photo card in the game.
